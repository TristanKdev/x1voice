# Where to show X1 Voice — launch channels, directories, accelerators

Ranked by fit for a **B2B restaurant voice-AI product at $250/mo with real POS
integrations**, not by how much traffic the site claims. Research date 2026-08-06;
prices and deadlines move, re-check before submitting.

## Tier 1 — highest intent: POS and platform marketplaces

Restaurant buyers shop for software inside the POS they already run. These convert
better than any launch site and are permanent (no 24-hour spike).

| Channel | Why it fits | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Square App Marketplace | Direct Square integration already exists | Requires app review; listing is evergreen |
| Clover App Market | Direct Clover integration exists | Revenue share applies |
| Toast Partner Ecosystem | Reached via Deliverect today | Partner tier gates the listing |
| Deliverect / Otter partner directories | We ride this rail already | Ask the partner manager for a listing |
| Lightspeed, SpotOn, Revel, TouchBistro partner pages | Same argument per POS | Usually a partner-manager email, not a form |

## Tier 3 — launch platforms (spike traffic, backlinks, social proof)

Never rely on one. Pick 3–4 and stagger them over a quarter.

- **Product Hunt** — still the biggest single day; needs a hunter, assets, and a warm list
- **Uneed** — Product Hunt-style, easier to win a badge
- **BetaList** — early-adopter list-building, best pre-launch
- **Fazier**, **Smol Launch** (7-day visibility windows), **Launching.co** (hand-curated), **StartupBase**, **Peerlist Launch**
- **Hacker News (Show HN)** — only with a technical angle (latency, POS sync failure modes); the blog's engineering posts are the hook
- **Indie Hackers**, **r/restaurateur**, **r/smallbusiness**, **r/KitchenConfidential** — community, not launch; contribute before posting

## Tier 4 — evergreen directories (SEO + AI-answer citations)

These feed the GEO/AEO work already in the blog: assistants quote directories.

- **G2**, **Capterra / GetApp / Software Advice** (Gartner network) — the ones restaurant buyers actually search; review volume is the lever
- **SaaSHub**, **AlternativeTo** — rank for "X alternative" queries
- **There's An AI For That** — free listing path once verified
- **Futurepedia** — paid submission, ~$197
- **FutureTools.io**, **aitools.fyi**, **Best AI Brands**
- **Crunchbase**, **F6S**, **AngelList/Wellfound** — investor-side discoverability

## Tier 5 — accelerators / capital (if you want the money and the network)

- **Branded Hospitality Ventures** (hospitality operators as LPs — the network is the point)
- **Techstars** (check food/retail-tech verticals per cohort)
- **Y Combinator** (twice-yearly batches)
- **Venture Hive** (hospitality among its sectors)
- **EIT Food Accelerator Network** (EU, food-system focused)
- **Downtown Project** (hospitality + community, Las Vegas)

## Sequencing that actually works

1. **Now**: POS marketplaces + G2/Capterra listings, and start collecting reviews from the
   five verified customers. Evergreen, compounding, no deadline.
2. **Next 30 days**: BetaList + Uneed + Fazier as a warm-up, then Product Hunt with real
   assets (demo-line recording is the differentiator — nobody else lets you call the product).
3. **Quarterly**: one Show HN or trade-press story built on a specific technical post from
   the blog, not on the launch itself.
4. **Book the season**: MURTEC Start-Up Alley (March) and NRA Show Startup Alley (May) have
   long lead times and cost real money — decide by fall.

## The asset every one of these needs

A 60-second demo recording of a real call, the public demo line, one screenshot of a POS
ticket produced from a call, and one number you can defend. The demo line is the strongest
launch asset here: "call this number and order like a customer" is a proof no competitor's
landing page offers.
